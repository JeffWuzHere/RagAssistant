# Knowledge Base Entry: Not all products appearing in feed — misconfigured 'Price less than' filter silently excluding products

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** One Google Shopping feed was missing products while other feeds on the same store worked fine. The customer suspected a misconfiguration but couldn't locate it. A "Price less than" filter had an incorrect threshold set, silently excluding products above that price.
**The Fix:** Identify the misconfigured rule/filter by temporarily removing all rules and filters, confirming products appear, then re-adding them one at a time to find the culprit.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** not all products in feed, some products missing from feed, feed showing fewer products than expected, one feed missing products but others work, products excluded unexpectedly, price filter wrong, rules excluding products
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. Ask the customer to open the affected feed's XML URL directly in **incognito mode** and count the product rows — confirm the missing products are not in the file at all (not just a display issue).
> 2. Go to the feed's **Rules & Filters** tab and **temporarily remove or disable all rules**.
> 3. Refresh the feed and check the XML again — if the missing products now appear, a rule is the cause.
> 4. Re-add rules one at a time, refreshing after each, until the missing products disappear again. That rule is the culprit.
> 5. Common misconfiguration to check first: **price-based filters** (e.g., "Price less than X", "Price greater than Y") — verify the threshold values match the actual product price range in the store.
> 6. Fix or remove the misconfigured rule and refresh the feed.

## 💡 Internal Insights (If applicable)
*This section contains logic extracted from internal notes or agent-to-agent talk.*
- Price-based filters are a common source of silent product exclusion because the threshold value can easily drift out of sync with the product catalog (e.g., if prices increase over time, a "Price less than 50" filter will start excluding more and more products without any visible error).
- The "one feed broken, others fine" pattern is a strong signal that the issue is in that specific feed's rules/filters rather than a plugin bug or WooCommerce configuration — different feeds typically have different rule sets.
- The fastest diagnostic is always: remove all rules → confirm products appear → add rules back one by one. This takes 3–5 minutes and definitively identifies the culprit without needing site access.
- The same pattern applies to any filter condition (availability, category, stock status, custom field) — not just price. When products are unexpectedly missing, rules/filters should always be the first thing checked.
