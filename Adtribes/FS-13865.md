# Knowledge Base Entry: GMC 'missing inventory data' on local inventory feed — feed XML is empty due to rules/filters; also GTIN/MPN/Brand required

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** Google Merchant Center reported "missing inventory data" for products after a new category feed was added and linked to an existing local product inventory feed. Investigating the local inventory feed's XML URL confirmed it was completely empty — rules/filters were excluding all products. Additionally, GTIN, MPN, and Brand were missing on affected products, which GMC also requires.
**The Fix:** Manually refresh the local inventory feed and open the XML URL directly in incognito. If it's empty, review and fix the rules/filters. Once the feed outputs product data, GMC will sync within a couple of days. Separately, add GTIN/MPN/Brand to products where required.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** missing inventory data GMC, Google Merchant Center missing inventory, local product inventory feed empty, inventory feed not showing products, new feed missing inventory, drawer runners missing inventory, GMC not seeing inventory
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. Ask the customer to manually refresh the local product inventory feed.
> 2. After refreshing, open the feed's XML URL directly in **incognito mode** and check if it contains product data.
> 3. **If the XML is empty:** the feed has rules/filters that are excluding all products. Go to the feed's Rules & Filters tab and temporarily remove or disable all rules, then refresh again to confirm products appear.
> 4. Once you identify which rule is causing the exclusion, either fix the rule condition or remove it if it's no longer needed.
> 5. **If GTIN/MPN/Brand errors also appear in GMC:** ensure that products have at least one product identifier set (GTIN, MPN, or Brand) and that these are mapped in the feed's Field Mapping. GMC requires identifiers for most product categories.
> 6. After the feed XML contains correct data, wait 1–2 days for GMC to re-crawl and sync the inventory.

## 💡 Internal Insights (If applicable)
*This section contains logic extracted from internal notes or agent-to-agent talk.*
- "Missing inventory data" in GMC for a local inventory feed is almost always caused by the feed XML being empty — GMC is receiving the feed URL but getting no product rows. The first diagnostic step should always be to visit the feed URL directly in incognito.
- An empty feed XML when rules/filters are present means at least one rule is excluding all products. The fastest debug is to temporarily remove all rules, refresh, and confirm products appear — then re-add rules one at a time to find the culprit.
- GTIN/MPN/Brand are required by GMC for most product categories. Missing identifiers cause separate "item disapproved" or "missing attribute" warnings — distinct from the "missing inventory data" error, which is specifically about the local inventory feed being empty.
- When a customer adds a new category to an existing store and links it to a local inventory feed with existing rules, the new category's products may not match the existing rule conditions — causing them to be silently excluded.
