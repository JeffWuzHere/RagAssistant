# Knowledge Base Entry: License account recovery and transfer after employee departure

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** A company co-owner needed to recover and transfer a product feed license held under a departed employee's account (tied to that employee's email), as they no longer had access to the login credentials.
**The Fix:** Support verified that both the departed employee and the requesting co-owner were on the same company website, then offered to manually transfer the departed employee's licenses to the co-owner's existing account.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** license recovery, transfer license to new account, employee left company, lost license access, can't log in to license account, account ownership change, recover license credentials
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. Ask the customer to provide proof of connection to the original account holder (e.g., same company domain, company registration documents).
> 2. Check the license records for the original email to confirm the license is real and what it covers.
> 3. Check if the requesting person already has an existing account with the same company domain.
> 4. If both accounts share the same company/domain and ownership can be reasonably verified, offer to merge/transfer licenses from the old account to the new account.
> 5. If the customer has access to the original email, direct them to the password reset page first as the simplest option.
