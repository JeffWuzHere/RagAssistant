# Knowledge Base Entry: VAT not included in feed prices despite WooCommerce VAT configuration — use System Price field

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** After enabling VAT on WooCommerce, feed prices were not reflecting VAT regardless of which price mappable field was selected.
**The Fix:** Map the price attribute to the **"System Price"** mappable field, which respects WooCommerce's tax configuration and outputs the correct tax-inclusive price.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** VAT not in feed price, feed price not including tax, price without VAT in feed, wrong price in Google feed, feed price lower than website price, tax not applied to feed, WooCommerce VAT feed
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. In the feed's **Field Mapping** tab, locate the price attribute row (e.g., `g:price` or `price`).
> 2. Change the mapped field from "Price", "Price incl. VAT", or any other price variant to **"System Price"**.
> 3. Save the feed settings and click **Refresh**.
> 4. Open the XML file in incognito and verify the prices now match the frontend (VAT-inclusive) prices.

## 💡 Internal Insights (If applicable)
*This section contains logic extracted from internal notes or agent-to-agent talk.*
- "System Price" is the most reliable price field — it retrieves the price exactly as WooCommerce calculates it for the current tax configuration, including any tax class adjustments.
- "Price incl. VAT on the front end" and similar fields can behave unexpectedly when WooCommerce tax settings change mid-feed-lifecycle or when non-standard tax classes are in use.
- For stores with mixed tax rates (e.g., standard, reduced, zero-rated), "System Price" is still the recommended field as it reads the per-product calculated price.
- This fix is also relevant when switching between tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing modes in WooCommerce — always revert to "System Price" if prices seem off after a tax configuration change.
