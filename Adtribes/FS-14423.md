# Knowledge Base Entry: TranslatePress integration moved to higher plan tier — Growth plan customers grandfathered

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** A customer purchased the Growth plan specifically for the TranslatePress integration, which was subsequently moved to the Plus/Business plan tier, and requested a refund since the plugin no longer served their purpose.
**The Fix:** The customer's Growth plan license was confirmed to be grandfathered — they will retain access to the TranslatePress integration and will receive the Translation Addon download link and license key when released, without needing to upgrade.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** TranslatePress integration moved to higher tier, Growth plan and TranslatePress, grandfathered license, refund because of TranslatePress, Translation Addon, plan tier change
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. Confirm the customer holds an active Growth plan license.
> 2. Reassure them that their license is grandfathered — they are not required to upgrade to access the TranslatePress integration.
> 3. Inform them they will receive a download link and license key for the Translation Addon when it is released.
> 4. Note: if their Growth plan subscription lapses and they need to re-subscribe, they would need to purchase the Plus or Business plan to access the TranslatePress feature at that point.
> 5. If they still insist on a refund, escalate per standard refund policy.
