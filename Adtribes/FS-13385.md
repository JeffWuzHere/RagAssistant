# Knowledge Base Entry: Feed stuck at "processing 0%" then immediately ready — caused by outdated Product Feed Elite version

## 🎯 Executive Summary
**The Issue:** The customer's feed was no longer updating or adding new products since a date in January; manual refresh would show "processing 0%" and then jump immediately to "ready" without processing anything.
**The Fix:** The customer was on a very outdated version of Product Feed Elite (v4.X). Updating to the latest version (v5.0.6) resolved the issue entirely.

---

## 🛠 Final Resolution (The "2nd Me" Guide)
> [!TIP]
> **Use this solution if a customer asks about:** feed stuck at 0%, feed processes instantly without adding products, feed not updating since a specific date, manual refresh does nothing, feed shows ready immediately after 0%
>
> **Steps to solve:**
> 1. Check the customer's plugin version by asking for a debug log or having them navigate to Plugins > Installed Plugins. Look at the version of both Product Feed PRO and Product Feed Elite.
> 2. If they are on v4.X of Elite, they are significantly behind — current version is v5.0.6.
> 3. Direct them to download the latest version from https://adtribes.io/my-account/ or provide the direct zip link.
> 4. Update Product Feed Elite AND Product Feed Pro (both are required).
> 5. After updating, run a manual refresh on the feed and confirm it processes products correctly.
> 6. **Important:** After updating from a major version (v4 to v5), there is a known bug where random empty feeds may be auto-generated. Refer the customer to: https://adtribes.io/knowledge-base/how-to-stop-automatic-blank-feed-generation/

## 💡 Internal Insights (If applicable)
*This section contains logic extracted from internal notes or agent-to-agent talk.*
- The "processing 0% → ready instantly" symptom without any debug log errors strongly indicates a version mismatch or stale plugin state. Always check the installed version before doing any deep debugging.
- v4.X to v5.X was a significant architectural update. Customers who haven't updated in a long time (especially those who purchased years ago with a now-expired license) may be sitting on these old versions without any in-dashboard update notification.
- Customers on expired licenses will also not receive update notifications — they need to log into adtribes.io/my-account/ directly to download the latest zip.
